Metric definitions and conventions

The portfolio weights and Kelly geometry section uses the asset covariance model and matches the geometry plot. The remaining metrics follow Trading Strategy Analysis Studio, using realized, periodically rebalanced returns and a 0% cash rate. Return-based model estimates can differ from the covariance-based geometry values.

Volatility uses sample variance. Sharpe, Sortino, Omega, and historical tail risk use simple returns. Sortino includes all periods in its denominator. Drawdowns start from equity of 1; longest duration includes recovery and any ongoing drawdown. Model curves require positive log drift and nonzero volatility.

Annualization uses the data's ticks per year. Rolling statistics require a complete observation window. Calendar-month statistics use UTC dates, including partial months. Sample dates start on the selected date and advance by 365 days divided by ticks per year; Binance and CSV keep their original dates. Undefined ratios and invalid portfolio histories show n/a.
